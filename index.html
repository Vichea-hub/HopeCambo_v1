Project Milestone & Execution Timeline

How every HopeCambo initiative moves transparently from local assessment to sustained impact.

Step 01

Community Need & Hydro Survey

Collaborating with local commune councils, testing groundwater tables, and ensuring priority for marginalized families.

Step 02

Solar Infrastructure & Drilling

Procuring durable local parts, drilling deep potable wells, and installing renewable solar-powered filtration stations.

Step 03

Committee Training & Tech Setup

Equipping village teachers with tablets, training local technicians for repairs, and ensuring water safety monitoring.

Step 04

Independent Audit & Sustainment

Quarterly water quality tests, open financial disclosures to donors, and full community maintenance ownership.
