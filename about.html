Who We Are

Dedicated to creating lasting impact through transparent and community-led solutions in Cambodia.

Our Mission

Creating Sustainable Futures, Together

Founded in 2018, HopeCambo operates with a single conviction: long-term poverty alleviation requires local community ownership, sustainable infrastructure, and total financial clarity.

We work directly alongside Cambodian village leaders, educators, and local authorities to build clean water wells, equip rural classrooms, and ensure food security for generations to come.

Financial Transparency

We hold ourselves accountable to every contributor. Here is exactly where your contributions go.

85%

Direct Programs

Funds directly purchasing water filtration pumps, school technology, agricultural tools, and local labor.

10%

Operations & Safety

Covers water quality testing, technical monitoring, safety equipment, and logistics in rural provinces.

5%

Fundraising & Admin

Sustains transparent accounting, secure payment gateway compliance, and public community reporting.
